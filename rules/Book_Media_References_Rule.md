# Book Media References Rule

Approved by Benji on 27 October 2026.
Scope: all books, stories, writing workspaces and their reusable templates, including Benji Chronicles and Benji Ink.

Every image or media asset developed, generated, uploaded, selected or revised for a book must be added directly to that book's References during the same task. This includes cover artwork, character and setting images, maps, diagrams, audio, video, posters and promotional material.

References is the book's permanent media archive, alongside its research sources and notes. Each item must identify the book, title, media type, date, version, creator or source when known, and a durable file or link. Images should have previews; audio and video should have playable previews when supported, otherwise a clear open/download link.

Keep original source files and every developed version. Label the currently selected cover or media version clearly. Replacing a cover must not delete its earlier versions or remove their References entries. Do not infer missing provenance or dates.

A media task is complete only after its References entry is saved and verified. Publishing an image as a cover does not replace saving it in References. Future book templates must include the References button and this behavior. Preserve existing research notes, chapters and story data.

Rules are maintained in both Benji HQ and Google Drive under the existing universal rule saving policy. This rule is a required creation workflow, not a background monitor of unrelated conversations or files.
