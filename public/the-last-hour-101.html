Review and decisions

Proposal review only. No story changes approved.

1. Chronology: Carol takes the Stardust job in November 1966, meets Tony, and becomes pregnant by spring, yet Nora is born in 1966 and is 30 in 1996. Choose either an earlier relationship/job date or a later birth and age. Preserve the names. Nora’s 18/19 December last-seen dates also need one agreed sequence.

2. Opening and reveal order: rules promise three ordinary-caller chapters, but chapter 1 contains a supernatural warning and chapter 3 introduces the woman. Jo is called the entry point but first appears in chapter 7. Decide between a brief Jo opening and the current Hal opening. A fair two-women reveal needs seeded differences in knowledge, not an artificial ban on voice description.

3. Mechanics and responsibility: define call routing, connection windows, who remembers superseded files, and whether a changed file is evidence of causation. Ray dying after Lenny changes route does not yet prove one life must replace another. Explain Danny’s danger and the human choices behind it; 'no villain' does not remove responsibility.

Ending recommendation: B, The quiet one. Its silence fits the adult voice and gives Hal’s choice emotional finality, provided we first resolve Nora’s fate and establish why Jo can let the cases rest. A introduces a new unexplained duplicate laugh. C needs a rule explaining how Jo remains present after her ancestry changes. No ending selected by Benji.

Carol to Nora: keep provisionally. It gives Nora a personal stake and makes Hal’s refusal consequential, but fix the birth chronology and distinguish it from Jo’s parallel family dependency.

Additional checks: midnight to 4 is four hours despite the show title; explain the title. Identify Gabe, the man crossed out twice, and the brown-coat employer without adding unapproved biographies. Reconcile the deserted 1996 studio with the station ending in 1979 and preserved equipment. Verify station identity/frequency, historical anchors and police procedure before drafting. Avoid treating readership claims as established facts.

Next: approve a chronology repair, choose the opening, settle the phone and memory rules, then decide an ending. Draft only after Benji explicitly authorizes chapters.
