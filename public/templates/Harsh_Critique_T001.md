# Harsh Critique T001 · Revision 2

Purpose: demanding, evidence based literary review of a saved manuscript. Arabic and English reports. Critique the work, not the author. A second AI pass is not an independent human reviewer.

## Required source record
Record book ID, language, title, chapter IDs and revisions, review time and reviewer attribution. Include only saved text. Mark a report stale when any source chapter revision changes. Never claim to have read chapters that were not provided.

## Criteria
1. Opening and narrative promise.
2. Pacing, scene necessity and repetition.
3. Character motivation, agency and moral consequences.
4. Dialogue and viewpoint: Egyptian Arabic (zk5) for Arabic stories; English 2 voice for English adaptations.
5. Originality and cultural setting; American cultural adaptation only for English adaptations.
6. Mystery logic, clues, alternatives and reader fairness.
7. Dates, ages, chronology, objects and continuity.
8. Historical, railway, medical and procedural credibility. Flag research gaps; do not invent authority.
9. Ending, emotional payoff and unresolved questions.
10. Prioritized Keep / Rewrite / Cut repairs.

## Report format
State scope and draft status. Give a candid overall judgment. For each substantial issue, cite the chapter, describe the evidence, explain the effect and propose a concrete repair. Separate observation, interpretation and a proposed new idea. Severity: Critical, Major, Minor. Scores out of 10 are optional editorial judgments, not objective measurements. End with the three highest value repairs and a retest checklist.

## American English 2 adaptation rule (English stories only)
Natural, accessible American prose with atmosphere and varied sentences. Retain emotional function, not literal wording. Adapt names, setting, institutions, travel and historical constraints together. Keep Arabic originals and English adaptations independent, with an optional related work link. Historical uncertainty stays explicitly labelled.

## Workspace behavior
Compact Harsh Critique button with transparent red fill and red outline. Save bilingual reports to the selected DSL branch using its revision guard and history. Report saving never edits chapters. Existing reports remain available after subsequent reviews. Downloadable review brief and paste/save workflow are supported. Automatic AI generation requires a separately configured backend and must never be implied by a button.
