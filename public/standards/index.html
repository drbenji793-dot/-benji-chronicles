HQ publishes the current protocol for each language separately. Arabic uses the zk7 Language Lab and English uses its own _zk5e guide. Saved chapters keep their original method, guide version and variant. Offline and historical references are labeled.

  1. Open a book, then Review, then Writing standard to read its rules and download the full guide.
  2. Review HTML, review JSON and Current Book Brief packages carry the relevant operating rules and source version. New returned review packets reject a changed guide reference.
  3. Arabic variant follows the book or passage choice. _am is العامية and _f is المثقفة. Legacy E and F remain visible through those labels.
  4. Book facts and the latest explicit author decisions take priority. Style adoption does not approve drafts, rewrite chapters or run an AI service.
  5. Source versions remain independent of website releases. Older packets without a guide stamp remain explicitly unversioned.
