_razor · Occam’s razor
Use the simplest complete design that helps Benji accomplish his task. Remove anything that does not earn its place.

1. Give each page one clear purpose.
2. Show useful content and the next action first.
3. Give each action one obvious place.
4. Combine controls that accomplish the same outcome.
5. Remove unnecessary steps, repeated explanations and empty sections.
6. Use plain labels that explain the result of pressing a button.
7. Keep rules, logs and technical information out of the daily workflow.
8. Preserve saved work when removing its interface.
9. Use compact consistent rectangles, small readable text and no horizontal scrolling.
10. Before adding anything, ask what task it helps Benji finish and whether an existing control can do it.

Smaller fonts and extra dropdowns alone do not satisfy this rule. Simplify the workflow itself. Keep source checks, revision guards and recovery working behind the interface. Remove clutter without deleting manuscripts, feedback or recovery records. Apply to future features and reusable templates. Existing unrelated sites require their own scoped implementation.

Chronicles Review: one feedback list and two entry actions, Export for review and Add feedback. Export one file with selected saved chapters and review instructions. Paste or selectively import feedback. Keep, dismiss or resolve the author’s review task without implying manuscript edits or automatic repair verification. Completed feedback leaves the active list and can be reopened. HQ holds the full rules.

Acceptance: the user immediately sees what needs attention, the suggested improvement and the next action.
