# Reader v218 | Essential character role filtering and reader export boundary

Baseline: verified production Reader v217, merge f15d5c3b671d9f83d21a0fab97e004e5187c4a67.

## Shared Story roles
Story's collapsed source inventory retains only explicitly central or main characters, based on saved essential/primary flags, primary/major/main tiers or clearly named protagonist/antagonist roles. An explicitly minor or supporting tier is not elevated solely because the record has a description. All other active character records remain in the full Characters workspace, preserving their original IDs, identities, aliases, history and biographies. The Story panel displays counts without copying biographies, and source names remain links. No fictional characterization is inferred.

## Reader and author export boundary
An automated regression now proves that a regular exported book contains its saved chapter text and no private planning truth when such hidden planning was not part of the saved chapter. The separately scoped author review package intentionally includes sourced spoiler facts and states that it is a writer review containing spoilers. No existing reader export layout, chapter content, author notes or source download controls were changed. This does not certify that every saved manuscript contains no spoilers; it verifies the export data boundary and leaves literary reveal timing to the author.

## Tests and acceptance
Two new regression programs verify explicit central role filtering, secondary character retention, source links, exclusion of retired records, preservation of biographies, reader versus author source isolation, and untouched saved chapter data. All configured regression and edition tests must pass in Arabic and English before release. Actual iPhone Safari navigation, display, Share, download and reopen acceptance remain open. External ZIP parity and recovery remain open.
